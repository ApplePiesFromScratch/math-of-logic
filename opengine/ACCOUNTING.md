## Are they consistently accounting errors?

Yes, **if** the ledger is: every letter names a generator, a V it landed in, and a tick or a refuse.

Then “substance,” “field,” “state,” “spacetime,” “true probability,” and also `Reading`, `Q`, `the product rule`, `FORCED-as-true` are the same posting mistake: **output credited, generator omitted, host treated as ground.**

That is not a proof that the universe is a ledger. It is the claim that these *models* debug as books. The meter is stipulated. Calling the meter “what is real” is error P on our own line.
