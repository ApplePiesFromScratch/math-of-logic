## Constraint

Stability of propagation under constraint = letters keep landing in the V you still support.
If the terrain no longer supports the map, prune (`prune.py`).
“No leak ever” would mean V already contains every future write, or you refuse every write. First is host-as-ground. Second is a corpse that closes (`BREAK_LOG` empty V).

So: systems leak **relative to what they were supposed to contain**. The “supposed to” is the cut. The container is the noun.
