# Naming is a cut

Naming does not uncover essence and does not grant it.
It isolates some slots and ignores others so a distinction can propagate.

The ignored slots are the compression loss (COMPRESS.md).
The isolated slots are the V of the next G.
Stabilizing = those letters keep landing long enough to read.

A model is that cut, run as a sim of a reduced external. Prediction is the read of the sim, not a report on the uncut remainder.
