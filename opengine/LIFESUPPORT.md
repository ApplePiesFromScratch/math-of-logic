# Life support in modern math

A patch is extra work that keeps an output after G has already left V.
Doctor move: wrap, name leftover, grow V in public, change combinator, or θ.

## Diagnosis

Every row is the same illness:

1. G writes a letter not in current V (leak), or
2. two G disagree (leftover), or
3. a project killed a slot you still need (nest).

Life support does **3 extra books**: invent a letter (`∞`, `δ`, weak solution), identify two mints (`f` continued), or quotient the failures (a.e., null, “ignore measure zero”).

That is off-book μ, off-book val-eq, off-book delete.

## Reconfigure (relational, fewer patches)

Keep four moves only.

**Land.** Change combinator so the write is in V.  
Bits: `lei_xor` / wrap instead of host `+`.  
Z/n: wrap-Leibniz, wrap-jet, wrap-complex.  
Probability: listed Ω, product independence, rate conditional.

**Leftover.** Two G, two numbers, keep the difference.  
Riemann `1/192`. Geom `1/32`. Stencil−jet `1/2`. `aa†−a†a=1`. η vs Euclid `2`. Add-then-born vs born-then-add.

**Public μ.** If you need the letter, add it to V and rescan.  
Adjoin `i` only after `-1` is in `Vv`. Completeness is a μ, not a discovery. Cutoff is a V.

**θ.** If you do not want the letter, refuse.  
`P(B)=0`, `r=0`, `1/0`, `a|0⟩` already 0, chart pole. No Inf.

Drop: limit-as-deletion, silent completion, a.e. as free val-eq, `∞` as a point that was always there, renormalization as “the true finite part.”

## What gets simpler

- Calculus I: isolate + dual mix + rate. No `ε-δ` first.
- Product rule: stamp combinator. Bits teach the leak in one multiply.
- Integral / series: leftover stays on the page.
- Complex: pair + square-rule. `i` is not a kind.
- QM finite: C², Born is π, commutator is order-leftover.
- GR 1+1: η read + boost gauge + path-dep transport leftover.
- QFT: oscillator leftover 1 + listed modes + path-sum order. No virtual-as-state.
- Stats: rate and product. No continuum of outcomes until you μ.

Fewer nouns. Same G. Host named. Patch retired or promoted to a public line.

## Still hard (not patches :  missing G)

Nonlinear GR in 3+1, interacting QFT on a listed cutoff that stays finite *and* matches experiment, a log table that lands in Q without a host R. Those need new listed G or an honest μ, not another `∞`.

Doctor does not claim those are done. Doctor claims the current IVs (completion, a.e., zeta-smear, Inf) are life support on a leak, not organs.
