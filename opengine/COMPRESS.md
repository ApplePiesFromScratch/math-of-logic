# Compression plus bad books

A noun is a lossy compress of a write.  
An accounting error is booking the compress as the opening balance.

Human math is those two, iterated.

```
python3 piles.py contradiction.py foundations.py break.py
```

---

## Compress

| write | compressed to | dropped |
|---|---|---|
| `S³(0)` | `3` | 3 ticks |
| isolate + two meets + `+` + `/` | `f'=6` | pair, seed, combinator |
| merge in front | `2+2=4` | count 4→1, location |
| bilinear of two isolations | `η`, `ds²` | the two isolations |
| leftover of two compose-orders | curvature, `[X,Z]` | the two paths |
| `a²+b²` | Born / “the state” | phase |
| μ on a subset | `P(A)` | Ω, the read |
| 16 bit tables | “multiplication” | 15 other combinators |
| listed leftover `1/192` | “the integral” | the grid |
| many G | “contradiction” | which C1-C15 |

Compression is useful. That is why nouns exist. The loss is the slots and the mint.

---

## Bad books

After the compress, three postings (ACCOUNTING.md):

- **P** credit the noun, debit nothing
- **C** book the host as ground, or one combinator as essence
- **N** book two cuts as one glyph (`+`, `=`, `2`, `contradiction`)

Life support is more compression after the write already left V: `∞`, a.e., completion.

---

## Why it keeps working

Q and R already hold the dropped letters, so the next compress lands.  
Bits show the loss in one multiply: `(1,1)*(1,1)=(1,2)`.  
Piles show the loss in one merge: count 1, volume 4, same word `+`.

The machine still computes. The books do not list what the noun deleted.
