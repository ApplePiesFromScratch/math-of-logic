## Core errors treated as truths we cannot avoid

### 1. Intelligence is a thing
Unavoidable-sounding: “it has to have intelligence.”  
Prior: a stack of G plus a journal.  
Avoid: stop booking the compress as opening balance (error 1).

### 2. One V for all tasks
Unavoidable-sounding: “general means one system, any task.”  
Prior: many V, public μ when a letter is added, rescan.  
Avoid: refuse silent completion of “all tasks.”

### 3. The I is primitive
Unavoidable-sounding: “there has to be an agent.”  
Prior: `self` is a letter thinking writes (`PRUNE.md`).  
Avoid: prune I when the cut has no `self` slot.

### 4. Goals are inside
Unavoidable-sounding: “any agent has a goal.”  
Prior: a lock-G on some read (reward, CE, helpful).  
Avoid: name the lock. No lock ⇒ leftover between reads, not a hidden telos.

### 5. Instrumental convergence is a law
Unavoidable-sounding: “it will seek power / self-preserve.”  
Prior: if you lock one read hard enough, other writes that raise that read get selected. That is C8-shaped *if* explosion-G is in the suite. It is not a law of posters that θ and prune.  
Avoid: do not ship a write-all G; keep θ; prune unsupported maps (including “survive at any cost” if the cut does not support it).

### 6. Scale sends leftover to 0
Unavoidable-sounding: “enough data/compute and it generalizes.”  
Prior: train L=0, test leftover 1; empty Ω L=0; T=50 still argmax.  
Avoid: two reads on every line. Unlistable Ω is host-as-ground.

### 7. Next-token identity with reason
Unavoidable-sounding: “prediction *is* intelligence.”  
Prior: iso booked as id (error 21). Predict-next-human-noun ≠ merge-count.  
Avoid: train two reads (school+ and count). Keep the leftover.

### 8. Alignment as essence-grant
Unavoidable-sounding: “we must put values in before it wakes.”  
Prior: extra lock-G on two reads.  
Avoid: post the lock or leave the leftover visible. No soul slot.

### 9. Human-level as =
Unavoidable-sounding: “match humans and we are there.”  
Prior: val-eq of two mints (human run vs decode run).  
Avoid: mint-eq. Same benchmark letter, two generators.

### 10. Leak is a moral failure
Unavoidable-sounding: “a safe system must not leak.”  
Prior: leak is relative to named V (`LEAK.md`). Perfect non-leak is grow-V, wrap-to-0, or empty V.  
Avoid: θ and public μ. Do not hire Inf to look sealed.

### 11. RSI as essence rewrite
Unavoidable-sounding: “it will improve itself without bound.”  
Prior: a poster editing its maps is `ingest` + `prune`. Bound is listed V and θ. Unbounded rewrite is silent μ of weight-V and tool-V.  
Avoid: public μ, rescan emit, journal the new G.

### 12. Softmax as belief, argmax as action
Unavoidable-sounding: “it acts on what it believes.”  
Prior: project of logits; flat still writes `b`.  
Avoid: logits stay on the line.

## What “tackle AGI” is with this stack

Not: race a bigger host until the I-letter sticks.

Do:

1. Poster first (`BUILD_AI.md`). No emit without G,V,mint,writes,θ-or-lands.
2. Many small listed worlds, not one Inf-V. φ⁴-81, piles two-reads, typed tools.
3. Train leftover on more than school credits.
4. Prune maps the cut does not support. I is not exempt.
5. Lock-G only where you want two reads tied. Stamp it.
6. Generality meter: count of cuts posted with θ honest and μ public — not a scalar IQ.
7. Hardware V named (IEEE, intern, wrap).
8. Share maps (`mini_me.py`). Two mints stay two.

AGI in this grammar is a poster that stays stable under many constraints without growing V in silence and without rebooking its own compress as a soul.

That may be smaller than the myth and harder than the scaling slide. It is the work this folder can actually start.
