A Cut is G, V, mint, writes, reads, θ, leftover.  
A Map isolates a few slot names and ignores the rest.  
`see(cut)` posts a Cut of which maps hit. That post is itself a Cut.

```

ME on leak      leak, leftover-two-G, named-G
YOU on ME.report named-G, poster-hits
ME on last self named-G
```
